How to play the farming simulator

Enter your name and click "Enter"

Your goal is to reach level 10, which is how you "win" the game.

You start off with 5 wheat seeds, click them in the inventory to select them
Plant them on the plots of land and wait a little bit for them to grow

Each time you harvest a crop, it will give back a few seeds and some money and xp
You can use money to buy better tools, and some of these tools will give more xp and/or more money

At level 4, you unlock the carrot, which gives more money and xp than wheat does
At level 5, you unlock twice as much land

Thank you
